### Align icon to title

Aligns the icon vertically to the title, instead of the whole notice. Useful if the content of the notice is longer.

<span slot="fallback">Loading interactive example…</span>

```jsx {5}

	label='Lorem ipsum...'
	subtitle='...'
	type='info'
	alignIconToTitle
/>
```
